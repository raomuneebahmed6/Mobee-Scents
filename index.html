FAQ

Common questions, quick answers.

How long do Mobee Scents fragrances last?

Most compositions are built for eight-hour-plus wear thanks to high-concentration oils and well-balanced base notes.

What sizes are available?

Every fragrance comes in 30ml, 50ml, and 100ml — pick your size in the Quick View or product page.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours. Major cities receive orders in 1–3 business days; see our Shipping Policy for full details.

Can I return a fragrance?

Unopened items can be returned within 7 days. Damaged or incorrect orders are replaced at no extra cost — see our Return Policy.
