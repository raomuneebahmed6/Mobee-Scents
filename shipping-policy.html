Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

We aim to get every order packed and on its way as quickly as possible, without compromising on presentation.

Processing Time

Orders are processed and dispatched within 24–48 hours of confirmation, Monday through Saturday.

Delivery Estimates

Major cities (Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi): 1–3 business days.

All other cities across Pakistan: 3–5 business days.

International shipping is currently unavailable — we're working on it.

Shipping Charges

A flat shipping fee applies to all orders, calculated at checkout. Orders above Rs. 3,500 qualify for free shipping.

Tracking Your Order

You'll receive a tracking link by email or SMS once your order is dispatched. For any delivery questions, reach out via our Contact page or WhatsApp.
